Fact #001 · Animals 30 sec read

Octopuses have three hearts.

Two hearts pump blood to the gills, while the third pumps blood around the rest of the body.

And here's the strange part: when an octopus swims, its main heart stops beating. That's one reason octopuses often prefer crawling along the seafloor.

Source: Smithsonian Ocean
